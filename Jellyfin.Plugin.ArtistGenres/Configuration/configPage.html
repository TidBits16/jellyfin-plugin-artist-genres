Artist Genres

Pick one source. Its settings are the ones below, and that source's own genre names are what get written onto artists, albums, and songs. The other source stays saved.

Switching this only changes which section you are editing. The library changes when you apply.

MusicBrainz

Writes MusicBrainz genre names, most-voted first, in MusicBrainz's own spelling. Artists use their artist page. Albums use the release group. Songs take the album, because the tracks in this library have no recording id.

Discogs

Writes Discogs style names, in Discogs' own spelling. Albums are looked up from a Discogs release id, or from the MusicBrainz release already on the album. Songs take the album. Artists are filled from their albums, because Discogs has no artist genre list.

From discogs.com/settings/developers. Discogs rejects requests without one, and nothing is changed until this is set.
Styles are the specific names, such as Indie Rock. Genres are the broad buckets, such as Rock.
